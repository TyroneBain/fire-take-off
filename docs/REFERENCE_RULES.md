## Selection list and visibility follow-up — 2026-10-27

[Selection list](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/241123-understanding-the-symbol-selection-list) documents example rows with element counts, current-drawing match counts, multiplier and resulting totals, plus selection editing. Our initial list shows source thumbnails/location navigation; the other columns remain unresolved implementation work. [Hiding counted items](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/89688-hiding-counted-items) hides underlying source elements from display and further selection, with global T, per-heading eye controls and filtered Shift+T operation. Our clearly labelled marker controls affect overlays only and do not satisfy that entire workflow. Normal Countfire account session now displays login; public research remains available and no login/access-control action was attempted.

[Keyboard shortcuts](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/64523-keyboard-shortcuts), read 2026-10-27, documents Space panning and Space+1/2/3/4 for Count/Measure/Zone/Note plus Space+d drawing chooser. These are now implemented subsets. Our Ctrl/Cmd wheel mapping is an explicit original policy; complete reference shortcut parity remains unclaimed.

[Quantity multiplier](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/89890-quantity-multiplier), read 2026-10-27, instructs selecting a symbol including its quantity note and changing the factor in that selection’s edit window. This supports per-selection factors. Limits, fractions/zero handling, manual marks and reclassification precedence are not established by the article; our declared policies remain in ADR-0018.

2026-10-27: [Measurement multiplier](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/4953975-measurement-multiplier) describes selecting a specific measured length, editing its factor and saving changes. [Zone multiplier](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/2340057-zone-multiplier) applies a zone-name factor to every outline under that name across all drawings; Excel affected quantities are red. The articles do not establish numeric bounds/fractions, area/point factor support or rounding/composition precedence. ADR-0019 states our provisional policies. Public pages inspected through Chrome; no private drawings or source code used.

2026-10-28: [Drawing comparison](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/8146469-drawing-comparison), dated 2026-08-08, uses Layout → Compare → Choose comparison, allows drawings from other takeoffs, shows original-only content red/new-only blue/shared content black, and supports position editing, arrow/Shift-arrow movement, reset, cancel, remove and Save. It explicitly removes the comparison when navigating away. Logged-in demo confirms the Compare tab and chooser control; no comparison was submitted there. [Revised drawings](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/64566-revised-drawings) describes individual replacement or separate upload into an existing discipline, new discipline or new takeoff. Shared discipline selections can affect old/new matching; replacement alignment depends on matching page size/orientation. These are separate from visual comparison and do not establish automatic correction migration.

2026-10-28: [Overlay drawings](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/8355632-overlay-drawings) describes Layout → Overlay → Add drawing, translation by drag/arrows, Shift-arrow ten-pixel movement, zero reset, Escape cancellation, Enter confirmation and removal. Overlays are temporary and cannot be saved across visits. The supplied authenticated demo shows project/takeoff/discipline/drawing chooser levels; it was cancelled without selecting a source. The reference guidance supports other disciplines/projects. Opacity/tint/multiple-layer limits and rotation/scale controls are not established by these observations; ADR-0023 makes our initial display policy explicit.

2026-10-28: [Floor plans](https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/8200729-floor-plans) describes stitching split sheets from the same discipline, alignment by drag/arrows and visibility toggling. Navigating to another member preserves the spatial arrangement. This differs from temporary overlays and requires a shared coordinate system plus durable membership/positions. No floorplan was created in the supplied account; current implementation remains NOT STARTED.
