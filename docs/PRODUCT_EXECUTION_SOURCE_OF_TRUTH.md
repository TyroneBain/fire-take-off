# Fire Take Off — current product and execution authority
Effective 2026-10-30. This document resolves priority conflicts in older roadmap, prototype and parity notes. Historical documents remain useful as evidence but do not override this current direction. AGENTS.md security/privacy/IP constraints always apply.

## Product promise
A trustworthy, fast, independent multi-trade construction PDF takeoff and estimating workflow: open drawings, identify symbols and measured runs, review uncertain matches, preserve corrections and revisions, reconcile quantities, optionally price by zone/page/package and export to Excel or supported estimating systems. Apple-informed usability without copying distinctive competitor expression. One shared trade-neutral recognition core; trade-specific legends, semantics and estimating rules.

## Explicit current priorities
P0: Prove vector-based sprinkler symbol selection and counting on authorised real PDF drawings with independent ground truth, preserving raster fallback.
P0: QA precision/recall, false positives, correction time, performance, saved-state and export reconciliation.
P1: Robust revisions/recount, quantity traceability, review confidence, pipe/cable/duct measurement feasibility.
P1: Flexible pricing packages by zone/page/floor/system with no double counting, researched simPRO integration and reviewed sync.
P2: Multi-trade expansion, legend-assisted recognition, specification/schedule extraction, accessible Apple-inspired polish, customer validation.
P3: Collaboration, commercial packaging and broader integrations once privacy/recovery/release criteria pass.

## Current constraints
- The product catalogue is **deferred** pending owner-specific customization. Do not build it by default.
- Prior requests for Countfire-like workflow are about usability and familiar task order, **not permission to copy source, proprietary assets or distinctive trade dress**. Owner may approve original layouts through demonstrations.
- Do not silently replace working UI, quantities, persistent corrections or exports. No broad redesign while counting proof is incomplete.
- REA analyses our own repository; authorized Countfire UI observation is a functional benchmark only.
- No claim of parity, accuracy superiority or completed integration without measured evidence.
